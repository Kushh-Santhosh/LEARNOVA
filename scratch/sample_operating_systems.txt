# Operating Systems: Concurrency & Synchronization

## Process Synchronization
When multiple concurrent processes access shared memory, race conditions occur unless mutual exclusion is enforced.

## Critical Sections
A critical section is a piece of code that accesses shared resources. Only one process may execute within its critical section at any given time.

## Semaphores & Mutexes
A mutex provides binary mutual exclusion. A counting semaphore maintains a counter that represents available resource instances. Wait() decrements the counter, while Signal() increments it.

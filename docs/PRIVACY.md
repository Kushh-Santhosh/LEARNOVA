# LEARNOVA Privacy & Safety Charter

LEARNOVA is designed to foster a trusted, transparent, and private learning environment for students.

---

## 1. Core Principle: Guide, Don't Take Over

Professor Nova is an AI tutor that illuminates concepts and guides your actions—it **never takes control away from the learner**.

### Explicit Boundaries:
- **No Synthetic Clicks**: Nova points to elements with visual spotlights and beacons; it never triggers artificial mouse clicks or presses keys on third-party websites.
- **No Credential Access**: Nova never reads, stores, or inputs passwords, PINs, or security credentials.
- **No Form Submissions**: Nova will never submit web forms or finalize transactions on behalf of the user.
- **No Irreversible Actions**: Nova cannot delete files, modify database records without explicit user intent, or execute destructive commands.

---

## 2. Screen Awareness & Visual Observation

- **Explicit Notification**: Whenever Professor Nova is active on a webpage or observing your screen, a visible privacy badge is displayed:
  > *"Nova is viewing this tab (Guide, Don't Take Over)"*
- **No Persistent Video/Image Storage**: Frame buffers and screen screenshots are evaluated in volatile memory solely for DOM element bounding calculations and pedagogical reasoning. No video streams or captured screenshots are persisted to disk or cloud databases.
- **User Agency**: The student can dismiss Nova, hide the spotlight beacon, or close the companion overlay at any time with a single click or Escape key.

---

## 3. Strict Source Grounding & Academic Integrity

- When studying uploaded documents, Nova adheres to strict source grounding to ensure academic accuracy and prevent AI hallucinations.
- When researching public web topics, Nova retrieves only verified, authoritative documentation URLs (e.g. `docs.python.org`, `developer.mozilla.org`, `fastapi.tiangolo.com`) and never fabricates sources.
